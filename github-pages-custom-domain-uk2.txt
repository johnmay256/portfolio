
How to Set Up a Custom Domain with UK2.net for GitHub Pages
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Log in to your UK2.net Control Panel:
   https://www.uk2.net/Control-Panel/

2. Go to "Domains" and select your domain.

3. Click "Manage DNS" or "DNS Records".

4. Add the following A Records to point your root domain (e.g., johnprops.com) to GitHub:

   Type | Hostname | Address
   -----|----------|------------------
   A    | @        | 185.199.108.153
   A    | @        | 185.199.109.153
   A    | @        | 185.199.110.153
   A    | @        | 185.199.111.153

5. Add a CNAME Record for the 'www' version of your domain:

   Type  | Hostname | Value
   ------|----------|------------------------------
   CNAME | www      | yourusername.github.io

   (Replace "yourusername" with your actual GitHub username.)

6. On GitHub:
   - Go to your repository → Settings → Pages
   - Under "Custom domain", enter: www.yourdomain.com or yourdomain.com
   - Click "Save"
   - Enable "Enforce HTTPS" after the site resolves to your domain

7. Wait for DNS propagation (can take a few minutes up to 24 hours).

Your GitHub Pages site will then be accessible via your custom domain.
